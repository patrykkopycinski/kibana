NotDaybreak Business Demo — Run-of-Show v2

Updated for the latest build. Four acts, ~12 minutes: arrive to work already done, decide with the agent, put two keys on a critical action, then govern and hand off. Every click and question below was verified against this build. Screenshots are dark mode, matching the demo default.

Before you start

  1. Open the demo in its own window — it's hosted alongside this script. (This script is also reachable from inside the demo: Script in the left rail.)
  2. Reload right before you begin — a reload fully resets the demo state.
  3. The demo defaults to dark mode, matching the screenshots below. Toggle under Settings (gear) → Theme if needed.
  4. Sanity check: the headline has the subline "While you were away I resolved 2 threads on my own", and the foot of the queue shows a teal Resolved autonomously · 2 group.
  5. If anything gets stuck mid-demo, reload — you're back to the start in 3 seconds.

Act 1 — "While you were away" NEW

~3 minutes · The overnight digest and autonomous receipts

The point you're making: the SOC didn't stop when your people went home. Work that was safe to do alone got done — and every bit of it is a receipt you review on your schedule, not a page at 3am.

1
SAY"This is the analyst's morning. Six threads need a human. But look at the line under the greeting — 'while you were away I resolved two threads on my own.' The night shift happened; nobody was paged."
DOLet the brief breathe. Point at the subline, the sparkline summary cards, and the "2 in motion" chip on the Contain group — work executing right now.
The brief: overnight digest under the greeting, sparklines per decision group, "in motion" progress on live actions, and "Watched by" attribution on every card.
2
DOClick "review the activity" in the subline — it jumps to the teal Resolved autonomously group at the foot of the queue.
SAY"Receipts, not tasks. These never count toward the analyst's number — they're things Dark Watch already fixed, waiting for review. A forwarding rule that was exfiltrating a mailbox to a personal Gmail: detected, confirmed compromised, removed. Zero messages left."
3
DOClick "Evidence trail in the record · 4 items" on the mailbox receipt. The record opens on the right.
SEE"What Dark Watch did" with an AUTO-EXECUTED token and the allow-list note; a Reasoning trace of the queries it ran; Verification checks with per-item confidence; and Follow-ups it suggests. Point at the "View guardrail" link.
SAY"It acted alone because this exact action class is pre-approved — reversible, single mailbox. And it proves its work: what it ran, what it verified, and the questions a human should still ask."
The autonomous receipt in full: action, guardrail, reasoning trace, verification checks, follow-ups.
4
DOClose the panel, then click "Reviewed — file it" on the receipt.
SAY"I review it, I file it. And if I never get to it, it files itself to Records at shift change — nothing autonomous ever disappears unaudited."

Act 2 — "It needed me": question it, then contain it

~4 minutes · Impossible travel on cfo@corp · now with the impact map

The point you're making: when the call is ambiguous, NotDaybreak builds the case, answers your questions with cited evidence, and waits for your sign-off.

1
SAY"Top of the queue: impossible travel on the CFO's account, MFA passed in both countries. Travelling exec on a VPN — or stolen session? NotDaybreak won't guess. It says: your call."
DOClick Open in chat (speech-bubble icon) on the impossible-travel card. Point at the Impact map in the right panel: entry point, what's compromised, blast radius — each entity tagged active / exposed / caught early / at risk.
2
DOType, exactly: Was MFA re-prompted on the second sign-in? — Enter.
SEEAnswer: MFA satisfied via the existing token, no fresh challenge — session-hijack signature. Note the Evidence · Okta citation tag.
DOThen type: What's the blast radius? — Enter.
SEE3 active sessions across Okta + M365, cited Evidence · Okta + M365. Both questions move to "Answered" in the panel — and the red Revoke active sessions chip appears.
Two typed questions answered with citations; the impact map on the right; the revoke action unlocked above the composer.
Don't improvise the order. The revoke action only unlocks after two questions are answered. Ask the two questions, then revoke.
3
SAY"That's token theft. Cut their access."
DOClick Revoke active sessions → the approval gate shows the full blast radius → click Revoke sessions. Scroll to the bottom of the chat if needed.
SEEThe contained card: 3m 48s to contain · 3 sessions revoked · 0 mail exfiltrated · 24h monitoring. Status flips to Contained.
SAY"Four minutes from 'is this real?' to contained — and it keeps watching for re-auth from that network for 24 hours."
3b
DO(Optional) In the right panel, People → Invite → pick Maya Chen (IR Lead) to hand off cleanup.
SAY"Maya inherits the entire investigation — queries, evidence, timeline, decisions. Nothing to re-explain."

Act 3 — Two keys for critical, one click for scoped NEW

~3 minutes · Ransomware on Sales-NAS (two-person) · data staging on FIN-DB-02 (one click)

The point you're making: approval friction scales with consequence. A scoped, reversible block is one click. Isolating a file server during a ransomware event takes two named humans.

1
SAY"Mass file encryption on the Sales NAS — 1,400 files renamed in four minutes. The move is to isolate the server. But that drops file shares for the whole team, so this action is under a two-person rule."
DOClick Isolate Sales-NAS on its card in the Contain group.
SEEThe confirm sheet, ribboned ACTION · 2 APPROVALS — blast radius, and both approvers named up front: You (Senior Analyst) and Maya Chen (IR lead).
Two-person rule for critical containment — both approvers named before anything runs.
2
DOClick Approve — 1 of 2. The button flips to "Waiting for second approval…"; a beat later Maya's approval lands (toast: "2 of 2 — two-person rule satisfied. Executing.") and the card resolves.
SAY"My signature alone doesn't ship this. The IR lead is asked automatically, and the action executes only when both have signed — every approval in the audit trail."
After both approvals: the card resolves, the queue count drops.
3
SAY"And when the action is pre-scoped and reversible, it's the opposite — one click."
DOScroll to "Data staging detected — FIN-DB-02" and click Block egress on FIN-DB-02 → the blast-radius confirm opens in place → Block egress. Done.
SAY"4.2 GB staged for exfil, and it can no longer leave — while the host stays reachable for forensics. Two clicks, audited, reversible."

Act 4 — Govern the watches, hand off the shift NEW

~3 minutes · The Watches page and the shift handoff

The point you're making: autonomy isn't one global switch. It's a roster of watches, each with its own duty window, autonomy dial, and allow-list — and the shift ends with a handoff the agent writes for you.

1
DOClick Watches in the left rail.
SEEThe coverage graph: who's on duty across 24 hours, watch by watch, with a live "now" marker — and cards for each watch: runs, acceptance, autonomy level, data scope.
SAY"This is the org chart of the agentic SOC. Watch Floor triages around the clock but never acts alone. Dark Watch owns the hours nobody's on — 10pm to 6am — and it's the only one trusted to act, on an allow-list. Fraud is still a draft. Coverage, not a black box."
The Watches page: 24-hour coverage per watch with a live now-marker, and a card per watch with runs, acceptance, autonomy, and scope.
2
DOClick the Dark Watch card.
SEEIts settings: the autonomy slider (Suggest → Read → Draft → Act, set to "Acts · trusted"), the duty window (22:00–06:00), hand-off destination ("Receipts to the morning brief"), assigned workflows with per-workflow toggles ("Removes exfil rules · allow-listed"), and data boundaries.
SAY"Everything we saw this morning traces back to this page: the mailbox receipt exists because this watch, in this window, with this allow-list, was trusted to act. Turn the dial down and it proposes instead. Org guardrails cap everything — gated actions stay gated at any level."
Dark Watch settings: autonomy slider, duty window, hand-off, allow-listed workflows.
3
DOBack on the brief (NotDaybreak in the rail), click the people icon top-right of the queue — Shift handoff.
SEEThe handoff compiled automatically: decisions this shift, still in motion, waiting on a decision — plus a note field: "context the queue can't carry."
DOClick Send to next shift.
SAY"The shift ends the way it began — with the agent doing the bookkeeping and the human adding the judgment. The next analyst's brief opens with this."
The shift handoff, compiled from the shift's actual decisions — edit, add context, send.

Closing line

"One morning in an agentic SOC. Work finished overnight with receipts you can audit. An ambiguous call where the agent briefed me, took my questions with evidence, and acted only on my word. A critical action that needed two named humans. And the whole thing governed like a team — each watch with its own shift, its own leash, its own scorecard — ending with a handoff written by the agent, signed by me. The dial between 'it proposes' and 'it acts' is yours, per watch, per action. That's agentic security operations with the human in charge."
