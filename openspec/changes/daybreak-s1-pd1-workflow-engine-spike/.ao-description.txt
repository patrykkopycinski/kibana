## Why
The shaped BMAD session (da4f07f3) identified adversarial case A-2 as the #1 unknown to flush before any real worker investment: **does the Kibana Workflow engine support the exact step shape we need** (HTTP fetch + conditional guard + AI invocation with structured output)? If the engine can't express this shape, the entire S1 spike is blocked and we must find an alternative substrate before building PD-2+.

This plan validates that in the smallest possible end-to-end slice: a trivial workflow with the three critical step types, run once to completion, proving the engine's expressive power and flushing the integration unknowns (connector wiring, step schema, execution API) early.
